Roadster Run

Drive into the screen. The bottom steers, the top lights the thrusters, and an outside lane in the air is a barrel roll.

Keyboard: arrows or A/D steer, hold Space or ↑ to thrust, M mutes, R restarts.

One thumb

Bottom of the screen — tap the side you want, or slide. It changes lane the moment you touch.

Top — press and hold to thrust, or double-tap for a short burst. The tank is six seconds. When the flame turns hot, let go and it cools.

Barrel roll — be in the air, thrusting, on the left or right lane, and steer outward. It spends half a second of fuel and slips past a side hit.
