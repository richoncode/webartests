Roadster Run

Drive into the screen. Hold the top to thrust. Double-tap the far side up there to jump and roll across.

Keyboard: arrows or A/D steer, hold Space or ↑ to thrust, M mutes, R restarts.

One thumb

Bottom of the screen — tap the side you want, or slide. It changes lane the moment you touch.

Top — hold for the bottom and back thrusters. The tank is six seconds. When the flame turns hot, let go and it cools.

Jump roll — double-tap the top on the side opposite the car. It pops up, the side thruster fires, and the car rolls across one lane.

Tilt — turn it on from the menu. Lean forward to go faster, back to slow down, and tip sideways to steer. A hard tip is the jump roll.
