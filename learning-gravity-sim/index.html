2D Gravity Sim — Performance Modes

The same Newtonian gravity simulation, run seven different ways — same particles, same physics, same timestep — so you can watch how much of "performance" comes from data layout, algorithmic complexity, hardware SIMD, real multithreading, precision, and moving the whole loop onto the GPU.

How to read this: switching modes or the particle-count slider resets the scene to the same seeded initial conditions, so any two modes are directly comparable at a given N. "Physics step" time is the compute-only cost (what these modes are actually about); "frame" time also includes drawing the particles to screen.
