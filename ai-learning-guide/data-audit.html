Research Data Audit

Every company's own salary range, median, and role-count estimate — each with its own source (Levels.fyi, Glassdoor, or a disclosed job posting) and caveats, unabbreviated — plus a live search link, so every number here can be traced and checked, not just taken on faith.

How this is built, and what changed:
  • Every company row's salary range/median is that company's OWN real data — a Levels.fyi self-reported median, a Glassdoor company-page average, or a disclosed job posting's pay band. Where a company only had a thin sample (n=2–3) or an "average" rather than a formal median, that's stated explicitly rather than smoothed over.
  • Each specialty's header figure is a genuine bottom-up computation — the median of the real company medians in the rows below it — not a separately-sourced market blend. This replaced an earlier top-down approach that repeated one identical blended number across every company row (unbelievable on its face) and, in one case, asserted a "$300K+" figure with no citable source at all. Both mistakes were caught by a user, not found internally.
  • Role counts still come from job-board keyword searches, which are inherently noisy — the same search on different boards routinely returns very different totals for the same company on the same day. This is a single research pass from 2026-08-10, not a live feed. Every row has a "Search postings →" link — click through to check a real current listing yourself.
  • Orange text below a range/median is an independent second dataset (supplied by the user, sourced from Gemini) — a different set of sites, pulled separately, shown for comparison rather than as corroboration. Where the two disagree, that gap is informative in itself; where a company shows no orange line, Gemini had no usable data for that pairing either. Note: Gemini's own dataset repeats identical Google DeepMind and OpenAI figures across several different specialties, suggesting it's less specialty-differentiated than the proxy titles used elsewhere on this page — treat a repeated Gemini figure as a company-wide number, not one verified per specialty.
