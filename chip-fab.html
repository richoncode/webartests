💡 Key Strategic Insight: "Co-Existence over Replacement"

While Canon's Nanoimprint Lithography (NIL) boasts a massive **10x capital cost reduction** and a **90%+ green energy footprint reduction**, it does not completely replace optical ASML EUV. For complex, layered leading-edge logic (like CPUs and AI accelerators), ASML's spectacular **0.7nm overlay accuracy** remains mandatory to align the ultra-fine nanometer contacts across 80+ stacked layers. However, for **3D NAND Flash Memory**, **DRAM**, and **Optical Elements/Metalenses**, Canon NIL represents a disruptive SOTA baseline—allowing fabs to bypass optical EUV's immense energy grid demands and year-long cleanroom setup times to scale up production immediately!
