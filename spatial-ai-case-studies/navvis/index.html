Multi-Sensor Fusion

The VLX captures 1.28M points/sec via two perpendicularly mounted LiDAR sensors. This arrangement ensures 360° coverage of both horizontal structures (walls) and vertical features (floors/ceilings), critical for robust Z-axis tracking.
