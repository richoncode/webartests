2. Performance Comparison: MatrixScan vs. Legacy

Traditional hardware scanners (Laser/IM) are optimized for single-point accuracy but fail at spatial awareness. Scandit's computer-vision approach leverages the parallel processing power of modern SoCs to scan multiple codes simultaneously while providing spatial context via AR.

🚀 Throughput Advantage

MatrixScan can process up to 480 scans per minute in batch mode, a 10x improvement over manual trigger-based scanning.

3. Foundational Architecture & Core Research

On-Device AI Engine

Optimized CNNs for localization (identifying code boundaries) and RNNs for sequence decoding (OCR/text capture) running entirely on the edge via GPU/NPU acceleration.

Spatial Tracking

Maintains persistent IDs for every barcode in the field of view. Uses visual inertial odometry principles to lock AR overlays even during rapid camera movement.

Universal Symbology

Unified decoding stack for 1D, 2D, DotCode, and Digital Link barcodes, plus high-accuracy OCR for ID documents and shipping labels.
