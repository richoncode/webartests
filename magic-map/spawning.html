Spawning Rules

Magic Map should feel like a guided wander, not a random scatter. Squirrels appear along plausible walking routes, avoid private driveways, and while you are moving they only appear ahead of your direction of travel.

Walking Modes

How new squirrels decide where to appear

The game changes behavior depending on whether you are standing still, walking, manually calling, or in country mode.

Standing

Balanced nearby hints

When there is no clear travel direction, new street spawns try to fill nearby compass sectors evenly so the player can choose a route.

Walking

Forward-only breadcrumbs

After the player starts walking, fresh street spawns must land in the forward travel cone between 40 and 120 meters away. If no safe forward point exists, the spawn is skipped.

Turns

Left and right still count

The street graph may follow up to three public turns. A squirrel can appear ahead-left or ahead-right around an intersection, but not behind the player.

Manual call

One close safe target

The squirrel call asks the same safe-pathing sampler for one close spawn. It still respects road safety, driveway filtering, active population caps, and street spacing.

Street Mode

Street and sidewalk safety

Street mode follows OpenStreetMap public ways and offsets each spawn away from the centerline so a player is never encouraged to stand in the road.

Public routes only. Footways, paths, sidewalks, residential roads, alleys, and other public walkable ways can host spawns. Private access ways and driveways are filtered out.
Not in traffic. Every candidate must keep at least 7 meters from motor-road centerlines after the sidewalk offset is applied.
No clustering. Street-mode squirrels keep at least 80 meters from one another so the map points toward walking, not tapping a pile.

Country Mode

When the road is not the right place to walk

If the player appears to be near a faster unsafe road and has enough property area, Magic Map switches from roadside spawning to parcel spawning. Those squirrels are spread across the player's own land, inset from property edges and set back from road centerlines.

Trigger

A nearby motor road at roughly 35 mph or faster plus a parcel of at least 0.5 acres can activate country mode.

Placement

Parcel spawns use blue-noise spacing so each new squirrel fills a different part of the property rather than forming a cluster.

Safety

Country mode keeps squirrels inside the parcel and back from public roads, because the intended walk target is the property, not the shoulder.

Current Tuning

Rules snapshot

These values mirror the current JavaScript config so field testing has a concrete target.

walking spawn range40 to 120 meters from the current player position.
walking directionForward-only using the latest walking bearing. Behind-the-player candidates are rejected instead of used as fallback.
forward cone75 degrees left or right of travel, wide enough to include useful side turns without sending the player backward.
street trace depthUp to 3 public turns from the nearest walkable road segment.
street spacingMinimum 80 meters between street-mode squirrels.
street safetyMinimum 7 meters from motor-road centerlines, after sidewalk/path offset.
local bubbleStreet squirrels more than 240 meters away are culled so old behind-you spawns stop blocking new forward ones.
manual callAttempts one spawn within 25 to 80 meters, using the same street safety and 80-meter spacing rules.
hold callPressing the call button for 5 seconds clears local squirrels and repopulates from the normal sampler.
country spacingParcel mode uses its own 16-meter minimum gap and property-aware placement.

Testing Notes

What should feel different

In mock mode, walk north, east, south, and west for at least one spawn step. New squirrels should appear ahead or ahead-left/right near public roads, never behind the travel direction, and not stacked closer than about 80 meters in street mode.

Expected failure case: if the only safe route points behind the player, the game should wait rather than placing a misleading squirrel.
