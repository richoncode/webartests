Supply chains

Oil and AI

Two chains flow left to right, stage for stage. The narrow pipe is the constraint.

Petroleum flows from oil companies through refineries and refining recipes to gasoline, diesel, jet fuel, and plastics. Historically the binding constraint was drilling, and at another time it was refining capacity. AI flows from chip companies NVIDIA and AMD, through fabs TSMC and Rapidus in Japan, through AI models as the recipes, to chips as the products. Today the binding constraint is chip fab hardware: long lead times to build and bring new fab capacity online. Demand and competition for supply are not the biggest factor.

Biggest factor today: fab capacity coming online. New fabs take years to build. Demand and competition for supply are not the biggest factor. Industry structure, not a recommendation.
