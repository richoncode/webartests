## Note on re-reading
A read taken 11 minutes earlier differs in 53 bytes, at 0x00c036 and
0x00cb80-0x00cbbb. Both sit inside the nvs partition (0x9000-0x86000).
esptool hard-resets the board after every operation, the firmware boots and
writes NVS, so the nvs region changes between reads. Everything outside nvs
was byte-identical. Expect a re-read to produce a different sha256 for this
reason alone; compare regions outside nvs when checking an image.
