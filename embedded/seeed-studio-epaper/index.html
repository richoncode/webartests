Seeed reTerminal E1002

Morning Dashboard

Color e-paper morning dashboard on an ESP32-S3: Open-Meteo weather, rocket launches, calendar events, and reminders, about 170 days per charge.

Stable Starlink in review About 170 days / charge

What it shows

  • Weather from Open-Meteo, including the air-quality endpoint the firmware already calls.
  • Upcoming rocket launches from Launch Library 2 (thespacedevs), shown with a viewing window. The firmware watches Vandenberg.
  • Calendar and birthday events, from a fixed table in the firmware.
  • Reminders such as changing the water filter. In the firmware that badge runs three days from the first Sunday of the month. Bin night shares the same band.
In review — not in the tree yet. A Starlink satellite-train visibility alert showing time, elevation angle, and direction. Pass source and any thresholds are TBD.

Power

Reported battery life is about 170 days per charge. The sketch wakes on a timer or a button and repaints only when something on the panel has changed, because a full refresh takes 15–20 seconds. The interval in the firmware is two wakes a day at most.
