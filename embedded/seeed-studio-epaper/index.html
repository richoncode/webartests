seeed-studio-epaper Working

Working files for the Seeed Studio reTerminal E1002, a full-color ePaper terminal on the bench: a 7.3" E Ink Spectra 6 panel, 800×480, on an ESP32-S3R8 with 32 MB of flash and a 2000 mAh battery. The morning dashboard wakes on a timer or a button, fetches the forecast, air quality, and Vandenberg launches, and repaints only when something it draws has changed. A full refresh takes 15–20 s, there is no partial refresh, and the panel holds its last frame with no power.
