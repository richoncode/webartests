espRotary

In progress — light-control fix under test

Working files for the Elecrow CrowPanel 1.28" HMI ESP32 Rotary Display: a 240×240 round IPS knob (GC9A01) on an ESP32-S3-N16R8, with CST816D touch, a quadrature encoder, and five WS2812B LEDs. The control firmware uses the knob as a SmartLife/Tuya light controller on the LAN. The module is already a finished 48 × 48 × 33 mm enclosure; case/ is a tray for a cell and charger, and the module is the lid.

seeed-studio-epaper

Working

Working files for the Seeed Studio reTerminal E1002, a full-color ePaper terminal on the bench: a 7.3" E Ink Spectra 6 panel, 800×480, on an ESP32-S3R8 with 32 MB of flash and a 2000 mAh battery. The morning dashboard wakes on a timer or a button, fetches the forecast, air quality, and Vandenberg launches, and repaints only when something it draws has changed. A full refresh takes 15–20 s, there is no partial refresh, and the panel holds its last frame with no power.
