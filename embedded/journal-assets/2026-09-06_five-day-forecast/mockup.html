Layout for the reTerminal E1002 at its native 800 × 480, in the six colours Spectra 6 can produce. Hard-coded for San Martin, CA. Every box is absolutely placed, so the coordinates below are the ones the firmware draws at.

Today is the first column of the forecast strip, so today's high, low and precipitation come from the same array as the other four days — no second request and no chance of "today" disagreeing between two places. The launch band draws only when the sky is clear or partly cloudy; the strip absorbs whatever height the bands leave.
