  <h1>Smart TENS</h1>
  <p class="subtitle">
    Multi-electrode sensing and stimulation for large leg muscles (quads, hamstrings, calves):
    monitor EMG, flag outlier / cramp-like patterns, and optionally apply TENS to help the muscle
    leave a cramp state toward relaxation.
  </p>

  <div class="pill-row">
    <span class="pill live">Status: design</span>
    <span class="pill phase">Phase 0 → 3</span>
    <span class="pill">EMG + TENS</span>
    <span class="pill">DIY experimental</span>
  </div>

  <div class="callout danger">
    <strong>Not a medical device.</strong> This document is an engineering design for a personal
    prototype. Electrical stimulation can be harmful. Do not use on the chest, neck, head, or
    across the heart; do not use with a pacemaker / implanted stimulator, pregnancy, open wounds,
    or impaired sensation without clinician guidance. Keep stimulation current-limited, isolated,
    and under a dead-man / intentional enable. Treat all numbers below as starting points to
    validate on the bench with a series resistor / dummy load — never “tune on skin” first.
  </div>

  <h2 id="goals">Goals</h2>
  <ol>
    <li><strong>Sense</strong> EMG from multiple sites on large leg muscles (target: 4–8 differential channels + shared reference / bias).</li>
    <li><strong>Track</strong> per-channel baselines (quiet / voluntary contract / walk) and detect <em>outlier</em> patterns consistent with cramp (sustained high amplitude, tremor-band energy, asymmetry vs contralateral).</li>
    <li><strong>Actuate (TENS mode)</strong> biphasic constant-current pulses on selected electrode pairs to interrupt cramp / encourage relaxation, with hard safety limits.</li>
    <li><strong>Log &amp; review</strong> waveforms, detections, and stim events on phone/laptop for tuning thresholds.</li>
  </ol>

  <h2 id="modes">Operating modes</h2>
  <div class="grid-2">
    <div class="card">
      <h3>Monitor</h3>
      <p>Continuous EMG acquisition. Streaming feature vector (RMS, MAV, zero-crossings, 40–80&nbsp;Hz band power). Outlier score vs rolling personal baseline. No HV stim rail enabled.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <h3>TENS</h3>
      <p>User- or rule-armed. Mux disconnects sense path on active stim channels (or uses dedicated stim electrodes). Constant-current biphasic pulses; intensity ramp; auto-stop on timeout / release / EMG drop.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="callout warn">
    <strong>Sense / stim isolation rule:</strong> never drive TENS into an active EMG front-end input.
    Same skin electrodes <em>can</em> be shared (see <a href="#shared-iso">Shared-electrode isolation</a>) only through a
    break-before-make analog mux / T-switch that parks the AFE safely before the stim H-bridge engages, with
    clamps, series R, and firmware blanking. Phase 1 may still use dedicated stim pads while proving the mux on the bench.
  </div>
