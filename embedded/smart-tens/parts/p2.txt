  <h2 id="shared-iso">Shared-electrode isolation (sense + stim on same pads)</h2>
  <p>
    Goal: one differential pair on the muscle does <strong>both</strong> EMG monitoring and TENS,
    without frying the instrumentation amp or creating a sneak path from the HV rail into the ADC.
    The skin nodes are common; the electronics are mutually exclusive through a
    <strong>break-before-make</strong> analog path select (T-switch / SPDT mux per electrode).
  </p>
