espRotary

Light controller for 11 Tuya smart lights on the CrowPanel 1.28″ rotary display, over the local LAN with no cloud.

Stable b37 Idle sleep off for now
Local only. Bulbs are discovered by UDP broadcast and controlled on the LAN. Nothing here is a cloud account, a key, an address, or the contents of devices_secret.h.

LIGHTS ring

The LIGHTS screen is a ring of 11 segments, one per light. Turning the knob selects or adjusts. Touch toggles.

  • Green — on
  • Grey — off
  • Outline — unknown
  • Red — unreachable
  • Selected — thicker, white edge

Builds b31–b37

Builds b31 through b37 fixed the wake bug and added the logging below.

  • A light-sleep wake bug: the encoder IRQ was left level-triggered and caused interrupt-watchdog resets.
  • Reset-reason and coredump logging.
  • Per-bulb state logging.
Idle sleep is disabled for now on the stable b37 build. When it comes back is TBD. version.h in this folder still stamps FW_BUILD 30, and the control sketch here still describes an earlier group flow (LIGHTS / ADJUST). Treat b37 and the 11-segment ring as the bench report, and the tree as the working files underneath it.

On the bench

The display is an Elecrow CrowPanel 1.28″ HMI: a round 240×240 panel, a capacitive touch, a rotary encoder with a push, and five addressable LEDs, on an ESP32-S3. Pin map, the two display-power rails, and the case tray live in the notes. Those notes also describe an earlier sleep design; the b37 report above is the one to trust for what is running.
