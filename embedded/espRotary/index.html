espRotary In progress — light-control fix under test

Working files for the Elecrow CrowPanel 1.28" HMI ESP32 Rotary Display: a 240×240 round IPS knob (GC9A01) on an ESP32-S3-N16R8, with CST816D touch, a quadrature encoder, and five WS2812B LEDs. The control firmware uses the knob as a SmartLife/Tuya light controller on the LAN. The module is already a finished 48 × 48 × 33 mm enclosure; case/ is a tray for a cell and charger, and the module is the lid.
