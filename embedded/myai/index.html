Hardware

The board is a Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Touch-LCD-7B-C. The P4 runs the device; a companion C6 carries the radios.

ModuleWaveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Touch-LCD-7B-C

Radio SoCESP32-C6 — Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth
Audio in2 microphones with acoustic echo cancellation
CodecES8311

Display7″ 1024×600 touchscreen
CameraOV5647
Battery, enclosureTBD

How the board is split

Microphones

Two mics, with AEC, into the ES8311 codec.

Speaker

Playback from the same ES8311 codec.

ESP32-P4

Application SoC, 32 MB flash. Firmware 0.3.14 on ESP-IDF v5.5.5. The 7″ 1024×600 touchscreen and the OV5647 camera are on this module.

ESP32-C6

Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth for the P4.

Gemini Live

The voice path. Which model endpoint, and where tool results are stored, is TBD.
