Physics & Formulae

Maintain consistent material effects by matching the Energy Dose ($J/mm^2$) between different processing modes.

Scanning: Dose = (MaxPower × Pct%) / (Speed × (10 / LPCM))

Dithering: Dose = (MaxPower × Pct% × Duration) / (25.4 / DPI)²

Note: This model assumes 100% duty cycle within the pulse duration and ignores thermal diffusion time constants.
