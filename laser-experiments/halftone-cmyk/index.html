CMYK Halftone on SS304

A step-by-step progression towards full-color laser engraving on stainless steel using specialized oxide growth and stochastic/ordered dithering.

The Workflow

Step 01

Lithos Pre-Processor

Image decomposition & subtractive density mapping. Decompose source bitmaps into CMYK channels for laser calibration.

 Step 02

Spiral Dither View

Continuous-path spiral dither. Dynamic heat mapping, tonal density ramps, and LPCM (Lines Per CM) optimization for fiber laser oxidation.

Core Concepts

Explaining Halftoning

Microscopic dot matrix geometry. Understanding stochastic jitter, screen angles, and dot diameter (microns) for print plate calibration.
