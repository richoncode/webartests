What This Model Is Doing

The grid estimates scan heat density, converts that into an approximate oxide-film thickness, then maps the thickness into an interference colour family. It intentionally exaggerates the difference between precise focus and +7 defocus because that is the most important visual difference in the reference proof.

Treat this as a palette intuition builder. Real colour depends on cleaning, alloy batch, polish, humidity, scan path, actual optical power, and camera white balance.

How Speed And Focus Move Colour

Sustained Heat ?

Slow speed and dense 1000 LPCM hatching keep reheating the same neighbourhood. That lets oxide grow after the first bright flash has passed.

Defocus ?

At precise focus the same nominal power can be too sharp: high local peak, less stable colour. A +7 offset can broaden the useful colour window.

Oxide Refraction ?

Thin films start straw/gold, move through bronze and violet, then blue/teal/green. Too much heat dulls the film toward grey or ablation.
