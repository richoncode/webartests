Phone ↔ Camera Pairing

A web experiment after Richard Bailey’s US 9,162,144 B2. The phone reports how it is accelerating. The laptop camera tracks the hand holding it. When those two gestures match, the screens flash PAIRED.

DeviceMotion

Hold a phone in front of the camera

  1. Open camera.html on a laptop over HTTPS (GitHub Pages) or localhost, and allow the camera.
  2. Stand so your torso and the hand holding a phone are in frame. Start on Pose / wrist.
  3. Grip the phone the way you would for a photo, fingers wrapped around it. TRACKING and a high “frames with a point” percentage means the wrist is still found. LOST means it is not.
  4. Switch to Hands, then Both. Compare the two percentages. Pose follows the arm. Hands looks for fingers.
  5. Scribble the phone across the picture. The trail and the acceleration plot should follow the wrist. Motion straight at the lens is the depth axis the match ignores.
  6. Optional pairing: scan the phone code, tap Enable sensors, start the live link, and repeat the scribble until PAIRED. If the direct link fails, copy the phone buffer and use Match buffer. Both pages keep about 30 seconds.
