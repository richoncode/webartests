What this demo will be

DataMNIST digits, 28×28 grayscale, loaded from a public CDN and cached in the browser. The files are not vendored in this repo.
ModelStart with a tiny MLP or a small CNN. Presets are on the Train tab; neither is built yet.
StackTensorFlow.js from a CDN. Order: WebGPU, then WASM (SIMD if the probe passes), then WebGL, then CPU.
DrawWhite strokes on black, downsampled and centered to 28×28, then a softmax over 0–9.
StorageBest weights in IndexedDB. Run metadata in localStorage['browser-ai.runs'], shared with the lab hub.
CaveatAll of that is still ahead. This page only detects the browser and holds the layout.
