Color Space Optimization

Separating Luminance (Y) from Chrominance (U, V) to drastically reduce neural processing cost.

In this POC, we convert incoming RGB to YUV (Rec. 709). The target for the heavy 6-layer CNN is only the Y channel, where most high-frequency detail resides.

Channel Roles:
• Y (Luminance): High-res neural upscaling target.
• U/V (Chrominance): Lightweight bicubic upscaling target.
