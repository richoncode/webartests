How tags are decided

One row per technique. Tags are an estimate for one eye and one 2× pass, not a measurement on a headset. QC XR uses the Snapdragon XR2 Gen 2 and XR2+ Gen 2 brief line “Render: 2.2K × 2.4K @ 90 FPS”, read here as 2200×2400 (11.1 ms). The low-resolution grid is 1100×1200. QC AR uses the AR1 brief: binocular 1280×1280 at 60 Hz, an Adreno GPU specified for sparse content, and a whole-chip budget under about 1 W as reported by The Verge. The briefs themselves do not print watts. AR1 is not the 3.1 TFLOPS GPU, so neural AR tags stay not viable. The AR column shows what an XR2-class GPU would take at 1280² only so the arithmetic is visible. Qualcomm’s XR2 Gen 2 brief (87-73689-1 Rev A) does not publish GPU TFLOPS or Hexagon TOPS. A third-party Adreno 700 note lists XR2 Gen 2 (Adreno 740) at about 3.1 TFLOPS FP32, and gadgetversus lists 3,100 GFLOPS. One MAC is two FLOPs, so the peak used here is 1.55 trillion MAC/s. Neural XR tags divide MACs by 30% of that peak, inside a stated 20–40% utilization band, because a sustained shader rarely hits peak. Viable is at most 2.0 ms. Marginal is at most half of 11.1 ms (5.55 ms). Each neural cell also shows the peak time, the 20–40% range, and the previous unofficial 0.25 trillion MAC/s figure, marked “was”. The NPU column, “if QNN on Hexagon”, uses phone proxies: Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 about 26 TOPS INT8 and 8 Gen 3 about 34 TOPS INT8 (Wikipedia’s Hexagon table). Qualcomm’s 8 Gen 2 brief prints a 4.35× Hexagon gain and the 8 Gen 3 brief prints a 98% faster Hexagon; neither brief prints those TOPS numbers. INT8 TOPS are counted as two ops per MAC, with the same 30% derate. XR2 Hexagon TOPS are unpublished. These times are not measured on headset silicon and do not set tags. A 4× reconstruct on this page is two 2× passes and about 1.25× the MACs, because the first pass runs on a quarter-resolution grid. Shader rows are costed in texture fetches, not MACs. Edge PSNR and SSIM did not set any tag.

Portable cost model

Score another conv net from its layers and its weights and biases (W&B). MACs ≈ Σ over conv layers of (H × W × Cin × Cout × K × K). Optional bias adds are Σ (H × W × Cout). They are off by default so the count matches the MAC/pixel figures above, which leave bias out. Weights are that same Cin × Cout × K × K sum. Biases are Cout per layer. W&B is weights + biases. time (seconds) = MACs / (device MAC/s × utilization). For the XR GPU tags, device MAC/s is 1.55×10¹² and the tag utilization is 0.30. Anime4K’s ReLU is a positive/negative pair, so the plain formula under-counts unless those 3×3s are doubled, and the depth-to-space gather is extra MACs with no weights. GAN-S’s last 3×3 runs on the 2× grid (2,304 MACs per low-res pixel).

Worked example, Football ESPCN-tiny. Layers 1→8, 8→8, 8→4, all k=3: 1×8×9 + 8×8×9 + 8×4×9 = 72 + 576 + 288 = 936 weights. Biases 8+8+4 = 20, so W&B = 956. MACs per low-res pixel = 936. At 1100×1200 that is 0.80 ms at the 1.55 T MAC/s peak and 2.66 ms at 30%, which is marginal on QC XR. A plain four-layer 4→4 k3 stack would be 576 MACs; Anime4K CNN-S is 1,056 because three of those 3×3s are doubled for the pos/neg split and the depth-to-space gather adds 48.
