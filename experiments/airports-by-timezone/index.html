How presence is quantified. Data presence (0–4) from the latest hourly adsb.lol snapshot per airport: aircraft in 25 nm (≥50 → 4 · 20–49 → 3 · 5–19 → 2 · 1–4 → 1 · 0 → 0), capped when the median position age is stale (>10 s caps at 2, >30 s caps at 1). A rate-limited or failed check shows “—”, never a fake zero. Camera presence (0–4) from the researched verdict (24/7 or official standing stream = 4, weak/distant/test standing stream = 3, crewed/scheduled = 2, stills = 1, none = 0), adjusted by the hourly endpoint probe where one exists (HND, AMS, MAD, IST, MIA, IAH, GRU): Haneda's probe reads the still-image index, so its frame age is real; the others probe watch-page reachability, a proxy — not a decoded video frame. Local airport time is shown with each data snapshot; night/curfew hours legitimately read low.
