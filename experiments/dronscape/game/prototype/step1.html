How to play
  • Click a scout to select it. Click the map to move. Shift-click to queue waypoints.
  • Scouts reveal a ~300 m circle. Areas you've seen stay grey: terrain remembered, enemies not shown.
  • Beyond the dashed link ring a scout stops reporting. It finishes its last order, then flies home.
  • Batteries last ~6 min. A scout parked on the launch pad recharges. Empty battery = scout lost.
  • Find all hidden contacts before 8:00. Space pauses; you can give orders while paused.
