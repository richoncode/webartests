Supervisory Swarm C2

Sixteen agents. Mission-level tasking. A jam toggle. The swarm keeps the last order when the link dies — and the operator can see what broke.

Thesis: Supervisors task a small swarm at mission level; under degraded communications the swarm still behaves, and the operator can see what broke.
60-second walkthrough. Select Alpha → Survey ridge → flip Degrade / jam as they enter the orange sector → try Abort on a lost agent (command fails) → reassign the still-linked flights → scrub the after-action strip.
01 · Task

Click a flight, then assign a mission. Agents leave the FOB as a group — you are not flying airframes.

02 · Degrade

Flip jam. Inside the sector, links go degraded then lost. Last-order autonomy continues. New C2 may timeout or fail.

03 · See what broke

Badges, the status strip, stale last-fix marks, and the replay log show tasked vs not-delivered.
