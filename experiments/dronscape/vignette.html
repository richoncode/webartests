Supervisory Swarm C2

San Martin Foothills — contested corridor (demo)

Sixteen agents over public ranchland east of San Martin, CA. Mission-level tasking. A jam toggle. The swarm keeps the last order when the link dies — and the operator can see what broke.

Thesis: Supervisors task a small swarm at mission level; under degraded communications the swarm still behaves, and the operator can see what broke.
Demo scenario, not a real base. Fictional FOB / training-style layout on real public foothills and open ranchland east of San Martin (near Harvey Bear Ranch county parkland). Labels are invented. No military installation is depicted.
60-second walkthrough. Select Alpha → Survey ridge (northern grassy ridgeline) → flip Degrade / jam as they enter the orange sector on the corridor → try Abort on a lost agent (command fails) → reassign the still-linked flights → scrub the after-action strip.
01 · Task

Click a flight, then assign a mission. Agents leave the demo FOB as a group — you are not flying airframes.

02 · Degrade

Flip jam on the contested corridor. Inside the sector, links go degraded then lost. Last-order autonomy continues. New C2 may timeout or fail.

03 · See what broke

Badges, the status strip, stale last-fix marks, and the replay log show tasked vs not-delivered.
