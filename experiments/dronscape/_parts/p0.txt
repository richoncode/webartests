<div class="page">
  <p class="section-label">Feature Landscape</p>
  <h1>Dronscape</h1>
  <p class="subtitle">A capability taxonomy for combat drones, organized by what a feature actually <em>does</em> — human/AI control balance, fleet coordination, hardware, comms, regulation — rather than by which company or program described it. Collapsed, this is just a feature list; expand any item for its detail, source, and (where available) timestamp.</p>
  <p class="subtitle" style="margin-top: -14px;">→ <a href="./dronescape-planning.html">Simulator architecture planning</a> — tech-stack options for a web/WebXR sim that exercises these features against pluggable control models, including RF jamming.</p>

  <div class="callout">
    <strong>Company-agnostic by design:</strong> the categories and spectrum tiers below hold features from multiple programs. <strong>Human/AI-Hybrid Control</strong> and <strong>Fleet &amp; Swarm Capabilities</strong> are framed as spectrums with tier ladders; most tiers now have a real sourced example, one still doesn't (marked "Concept") — that's the taxonomy's own gap, not a claim that no one has built it.
  </div>
  <div class="callout warn">
    <strong>Three source tiers, not one:</strong> items tagged <span class="src-tag" style="vertical-align: middle;">Neros · ...</span> come from three full-transcript interviews with Neros CTO Olaf Hichwa (Oct 2024 – Aug 2026) — high confidence in wording, and a growing number of items are now corroborated across two or three of those interviews rather than mentioned once. Items badged <span class="badge research" style="vertical-align: middle;">Research</span> come from a separate, meaningfully less-verified batch — a research/industry-literature compilation built from search-result snippets, not fetched source pages, and not independently checked here. Items badged <span class="badge consider" style="vertical-align: middle;">Consider</span> (src-tag <span class="src-tag" style="vertical-align: middle;">Project AirSim</span>) are <em>simulator-platform</em> capabilities from Microsoft / IAMAISim Project AirSim (Unreal-hosted + Runtime), listed so Dronscape can weigh them for Track&nbsp;1–2 builds — not combat-drone field features. See "Sourcing &amp; methodology" at the bottom for exactly what that means before treating any batch as ground truth.
  </div>
