How to read this

Four lanes of one story: compute from a chip inside a single device out to a cloud fleet; multidevice experience from a companion screen to several people in one spatial session; authoring tools that make those interfaces faster to build; and software that drives something outside the computer. This is not a resume and not a list of jobs. Solid bars are sourced. A bar marked Guess is a best guess. Dotted cards are exploration or interest and sit off the axis. Select a bar to read the evidence. If another lane lights up, that is the same story thread. It is not a claim that one thing caused the other.

Guess means the date or the lane is still a best guess. Exploration and Interest only stay off the axis. A second outline is the same story thread, not a cause.
