What this is

The yellow line is the COTA centerline (5.5 km), drawn with a black casing so it stays readable. The car rides that loop on a 15 m asphalt ribbon with a concrete shoulder generated from the same line. Mapbox Standard still supplies the surrounding streets and 3D buildings. Local streets and minor roads are redrawn at real-world width in asphalt gray, with a light sidewalk on the curb. Time on the scrubber is a cached OpenF1 car_data window, and speed from that window moves the car. Distance is scaled by about 1.5% so the two laps meet this centerline. The OpenF1 x/y grid is not fitted onto the map, so a brake in the trace is not pinned to the matching corner.

Gauges and the synth read the same clock. RPM is interpolated. Throttle and gear stay as steps, and a gear change dips the sound. Brake, speed, and DRS are shown on the panel and do not retune the synth. The Engine menu loads a voicing from engines/. High-rev V6 is the default: the saw and square firing stack from the earlier Spatial F1 engine, colored with the live FireSim Audio resonator centers. Throaty V8, idle-heavy, and muted stay optional. The synth is Web Audio, a stand-in until a WASM Engine Sim can take the same inputs.

Chase is the default camera. The lens sits about 31.5 m behind the car anchor and Mapbox pitch is 85°, the most level look the map allows, so the aim is the horizon. In that view the model is 5.5 m long. Overview fits the whole circuit and draws the car at 16 m so it stays findable. Heading is a look-ahead average of the centerline, eased over a few metres, so a choppy vertex does not twitch the car or the camera. Drag to slide the chase view. Right-drag, or ctrl-drag, turns and pitches it. The camera holds still while the pointer is down, and on release it eases back to the chase position and heading. ?zoom=, ?pitch=, and ?back= (metres behind the anchor) tune that chase camera. ?head= adds radians to the model. The default is 0, which points the nose along the direction of travel.
