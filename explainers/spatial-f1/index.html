What this is

The yellow line is the COTA centerline (5.5 km). The car rides that loop. Time on the scrubber is a cached OpenF1 car_data window, and speed from that window moves the car. Distance is scaled by about 1.5% so the two laps meet this centerline. The OpenF1 x/y grid is not fitted onto the map, so a brake in the trace is not pinned to the matching corner.

Gauges and the synth read the same clock. RPM is interpolated. Throttle and gear stay as steps, and a gear change dips the sound. Brake, speed, and DRS are shown on the panel and do not retune the synth. The synth is Web Audio, a stand-in until a WASM Engine Sim can take the same inputs.

Cockpit is the default camera: low, a few metres behind the car, looking along the track. Overview restores the previous pitched map of the whole circuit. ?back=, ?eye=, and ?look= are metres for that chase camera. ?head= still rotates the model.
