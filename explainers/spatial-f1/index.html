Spatial F1 demo

A low-poly F1 car lapping Circuit of the Americas (Austin) in 3D on Mapbox. Unofficial; not affiliated with Formula 1.

What this is

The track line is the COTA centerline (5.5 km). The car follows it in a loop at a made-up pace (about a 1:36 lap at 1×, close to race pace), not real timing. The car is drawn about 6× real size so it reads at map zoom.

Next: real car positions from OpenF1 (2024/2025 Austin races report every car about 4 times a second, in F1's own x/y grid). Fitting that grid onto this centerline would let all 20 cars replay a real race window.
