Module 10: LeGEPA & Causal-JEPA

Stability and Reasoning: Transitioning from correlative patterns to causal understanding.

Causal Understanding

Standard JEPAs learn to associate patterns. However, true intelligence requires understanding Causality—the difference between "The grass is wet because it rained" and "The grass is wet because the sprinkler is on."

**Causal-JEPA** incorporates intervention logic into the latent space. By distinguishing between observations and interventions, the model can predict the results of actions it has never seen before, making it more robust for zero-shot planning.

Interactive Simulation: "Intervention Logic"

Causal Graph: Rain (R) → Wet Grass (W) ← Sprinkler (S)

In Observation Mode, seeing wet grass increases the model's belief that it rained. In Intervention Mode, if you *turn on* the sprinkler manually, the model correctly identifies that wet grass no longer implies rain. This is the foundation of causal reasoning.
