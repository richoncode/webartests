Module 05: VL-JEPA - Multimodal Grounding

Thinking in Meaning Space, Not Token Space.

Aligning Vision and Language

VL-JEPA allows an AI to understand the relationship between vision and language without converting everything into discrete text tokens. It treats text as a continuous embedding target. This solves the Symbol Grounding Problem, where LLMs know the "word" but not the "world."

By using Selective Decoding, VL-JEPA only generates text when necessary, staying in the abstract "Meaning Space" for the majority of its reasoning.
