Module 03: Hierarchical JEPA (H-JEPA)

Modeling multiple time scales: understanding both fine details and broad semantic concepts through a structured hierarchy.

Multi-Scale World Modeling

The physical world operates on multiple temporal and spatial scales. A "World Model" must understand that a leaf moves in milliseconds, but a season changes over months. **H-JEPA** adds a hierarchical structure to the predictive architecture, allowing the model to handle these diverse scales simultaneously.

Higher layers in the hierarchy predict slow, abstract representations (e.g., "The car is traveling south"), while lower layers predict fast, granular details (e.g., "The wheel is rotating at 10Hz").
