Module 03: Hierarchical JEPA (H-JEPA)

Managing complexity through structural and temporal hierarchy: predicting both abstract concepts and granular details.

Multi-Level Latent Representations

Physical reality is organized into hierarchies. To build a robust World Model, an agent must simultaneously process information across multiple scales of space and time. **H-JEPA** extends the architecture by adding multiple levels of latent representations.

Higher Levels (Semantic)

  • Abstract Structure: Focuses on objects, scenes, and identities.
  • Longer Time Spans: Predicts shifts occurring over seconds or minutes (e.g., "The car is turning").

Lower Levels (Granular)

  • Fine Detail: Focuses on pixels, edges, and textures.
  • Short Time Steps: Predicts rapid fluctuations (e.g., "The reflection on the window is flickering").

Level 3 Analysis: Concept Trajectory

Predicting the abstract identity of the car is highly stable. The model ignores irrelevant pixel noise and wheel rotations, focusing on the overall semantic goal.
