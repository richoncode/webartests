Module 04: V-JEPA & MC-JEPA

Moving from Space to Time: Video world models and disentangled features.

Spatiotemporal Dynamics

V-JEPA extends the architecture to video. To prevent the model from "cheating" by looking at adjacent frames, it hides the same spatial area across the entire temporal block. This forces the model to learn motion and persistence rather than just copying pixels from frame t-1.

MC-JEPA (Motion-Content) goes further by learning to disentangle Optical Flow from object identity using a shared encoder.

Interactive Simulation: "The 3D Masker"

The 3D grid represents a Tubelet of video (X, Y, Time). Switch to Contiguous Masking to see how V-JEPA blocks "vertical" tubes in time, forcing the model to predict what happened behind the mask using its internal world model.
