Module 09: Act-JEPA & Action-Conditioned World Models

From Passive Observation to Active Interaction: Learning the causal effects of agency.

Action-Conditioned Prediction

Passive JEPAs predict the future based on observation alone. **Act-JEPA** introduces agency by conditioning predictions on an Action Sequence. The model asks: "Given the current state $S_t$, what is the representation of the world at $S_{t+1}$ if I apply force $A_t$?"

This allows the agent to learn a Policy in latent space, evaluating the physical consequences of its actions before executing them in the real world.
