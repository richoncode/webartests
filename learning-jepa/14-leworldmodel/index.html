Module 14: LeWorldModel & Recurrent Planning

Actionable Intelligence: Transforming the predictor into a recurrent mental simulator for zero-shot planning.

The Recurrent World Model

Until 2026, JEPAs were mostly used for single-step prediction. **LeWorldModel (LeWM)** shifts the architecture toward Recurrent Rollouts. The predictor is applied repeatedly to its own output, allowing the agent to "imagine" the future 20, 50, or 100 steps ahead in latent space.

In this mode, the predictor becomes a function of both state and action: $P(s_t, a_t) \to s_{t+1}$. By evaluating thousands of action sequences in parallel, the agent can pick the path that minimizes cost before taking a single physical step.

CEM: Planning via Cross-Entropy

LeWorldModel doesn't just guess; it optimizes. It uses the Cross-Entropy Method (CEM) to sample thousands of "Imagined Rollouts," selects the top 10% with the lowest cost, and refines its action sequence. This process happens in milliseconds, allowing for fluid, brain-like motor control.

The CEM Optimization Loop

1. Sample Generate 1000 random action sequences.
2. Evaluate Predict outcomes and rank by Cost ($J$).
3. Refine Fit new distribution to top 10% (Elite).
