Module 08: Agentic AI & World Models

Planning and Model-Predictive Control (MPC) using JEPA.

Imagining the Future

In Mode 2 Planning, the JEPA acts as a World Model simulator. Instead of acting blindly, the agent "imagines" sequences of actions in latent space and evaluates their outcomes before executing a single move. It picks the path with the lowest Intrinsic Cost.

This is achieved through Model-Predictive Control (MPC), allowing for zero-shot planning in complex physical environments.
