Module 01: Escaping the Generative Bottleneck

JEPA models are "Observers"—they ignore the noise and focus on predicting high-level semantic meaning.

The Paradigm Shift

JEPA transitions AI from reconstruction (generating every pixel) to prediction (modeling semantic structure). This module demonstrates two critical advantages: **noise invariance** and **computational efficiency**. Effective agents prioritize features required for action over details required for visual fidelity.

1. Invariance to Noise

Traditional generative models collapse when inputs are noisy or occluded. Predictive architectures maintain stable representations by ignoring irrelevant signal fluctuations.

Technical Analysis: Invariance

Generative models (MAE) produce hallucination artifacts when data is sparse. They exhaust compute resources attempting to "guess" pixels based on local statistics. JEPA maintains Latent Persistence of object identity by predicting in abstract representation space. This enables autonomous systems to identify critical entities through rain or occlusion without the overhead of pixel-perfect reconstruction.

2. Computational Efficiency

AI performance is constrained by Resource Allocation. Modeling the world at the pixel level is computationally unsustainable for real-time edge agents.

Technical Analysis: Semantic Capacity

Efficiency is maximized through Semantic Compression. Representing a complex structure (e.g., a tree) as a single high-level vector preserves TFLOPS for high-priority tasks like hazard detection. Generative models encounter hardware bottlenecks by processing high-frequency details that are irrelevant to the agent's navigation or safety goals.
